VidLii

VidLii is an online video-sharing website inspired by the classic era of video-sharing websites. Users can upload videos, create channels, subscribe to other users, and interact with the community.

History

VidLii was founded in December 2015. Before becoming VidLii, the project was known by names including ClipBits and VidBit. The current VidLii service launched in June 2017.

The website became known for its nostalgic design and its recreation of features associated with older video-sharing sites.

Features

  • Uploading and sharing videos
  • Creating personal channels
  • Subscribing to other channels
  • Commenting on videos
  • Favoriting videos
  • Video tags
  • Video embedding
  • Customizable themes

Community

VidLii has a community-oriented approach that allows users to create channels, share videos, and interact with other members. The platform has its own rules and community guidelines governing content and user behavior.

Legacy

VidLii is notable for preserving the look and feel of older video-sharing websites. Its nostalgic interface and independent community have made it a popular destination for users interested in the classic web experience.
